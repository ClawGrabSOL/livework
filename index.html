THE LORE

Why we made this

Pump.fun livestreams turned into the most-watched corner of Solana — but bounties stayed off-stream, off-chain, and off-vibe. We wanted a platform where every challenge happens on a live stream, under the STREAMWORK token, with the SOL escrowed before the camera turns on. No proof, no payout.
PROBLEM

Bounty boards reward edited clips. Streams get the attention but none of the rewards. The most viral moments earn $0.

ANSWER

Live-only bounties. Open a pump.fun stream on $STREAMWORK, complete the challenge on cam, and the smart-contract releases SOL the instant the team signs off.
