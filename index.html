THE LORE

Why we made this

Bounty boards reward edited clips. Streams get attention but no rewards. We wanted a platform where every task is a 1v1 duel, both duelists ante SOL into escrow, and the fastest valid video proof takes the entire pot. No team votes, no edits, no participation trophies.
PROBLEM

Solo bounty boards reward edited clips. There's no urgency, no opponent, no skin in the game. Anyone can take 4 weeks to film a "best take."

ANSWER

Two slots, two antes, one clock. Both duelists lock SOL in escrow. First valid single-take video wins the pot — loser walks empty.
